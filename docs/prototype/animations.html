Animation ideas: making the UI feel like it's thinking

Each one has a Replay button. Jev answers in about 100 ms, so we don't need spinners waiting on the model. Motion here is for showing what got decided and where it went. Tell me which ones feel right and which feel like too much.

1 · Card arrives

Rises, unblurs and settles with a small spring when Jev needs you.

Your September statement is ready

2 · Confidence fills in

Bars grow one after another, so you can see Jev's probabilities.

5 · Auto-decisions stream past

Jev clears the easy ones on its own while you watch. A dashed row means it stopped to ask you.

6 · Important pulses once

A single red ring, the background warms, then the short summary lines appear one by one. It doesn't keep flashing.

7 · Batch progress ring

Fills by bucket as the batch runs, then shrinks into the summary when the batch is done.

8 · Card changes shape mid-question

If your answer brings up a follow-up question, the same card changes shape to ask it. It doesn't close and open a new one.
